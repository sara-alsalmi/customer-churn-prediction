04The Clues
Who says yes? Six clues.
from the data · click a card to open it

Press → to open each clue, or click any card on the board. The dashed line is the overall rate (11.3%). Orange bars are above it. Hover a bar for the number of clients.

1 Past success 65%. 2 Mobile 2.8× landline. 3 May: busiest month, worst rate. 4 Each extra call lowers the odds. 5 Yes ≈10× more common in low-employment quarters (a pattern in time, not a cause). 6 Students and retirees are the most open.
