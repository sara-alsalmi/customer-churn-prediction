Sc. 10What It Means

From a call list to a ranking.

Today: dial in list order50 calls · real rate ≈ 6 yes
With a model: dial the likeliest firstillustration, not a result

Same people, same six yes. A ranking does not create them. It moves them to the front of the list.

The model ranks. People decide.

It gives each client a probability. Agents and managers choose who to call, when, and how often.

It does not create a yes.

It can help spend calls where they are most likely to matter. It cannot change a client’s mind.

It must be re-checked.

It learned from 2008–2010, during a financial crisis. The economy moves, and the model must be tested on new campaigns.

Explain the use: a probability per client turns a list into a ranking. The top row is today, with yes calls scattered through the list. The bottom row is an illustration of what a good ranking does: most yes calls move to the front. It is not a measured result.

Be honest about the limits: the model supports decisions, it doesn't make anyone subscribe, and it was trained during an unusual economic period.
